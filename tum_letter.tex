\textbf{Application for PhD Position: Data-Centric Research on Foundation Models}

\vspace{4mm}

Dear Prof.\ Heckel,

I am writing to apply for the doctoral position on data-centric research for foundation models in your group.

Over the past few years, I have spent most of my time building deep learning systems and implementing architectures from scratch. I developed TorchDiff, a PyTorch library for diffusion models (DDPM, DDIM, SDE, latent diffusion) that has been downloaded around 5,000 times. I also built CppNet, a complete deep learning framework in C++ with CUDA support, and implemented models like GPT-2, various CNNs, RNNs, and Transformers from the ground up. This kind of hands-on work has given me a solid understanding of how these models work under the hood, from the math to the engineering.

My Master's thesis at the University of Stuttgart was on automated design of gene regulatory networks, combining evolutionary algorithms with gradient-based optimization over PDE models. So I have experience on the computational biology side as well, though my main focus and energy in recent years has been deep learning.

I am genuinely interested in the questions your group works on. How training data affects what foundation models learn is something I find important and underexplored, and I would like the chance to study it properly in a research setting. I think my experience building and working with these models would be a useful starting point for that.

I would be happy to discuss my background further. Thank you for your time.
